<h1 class="serif">Clinically Integrated Networks under the Kansas RHTP</h1>
<p>Four networks matter to this program: two that formed under Year 1 awards — the Kansas High Value Network and PrairieLINK Health Alliance; the newest, Astra Health, a shared-services organization announced September 8, 2026 whose five founders include The University of Kansas Health System and which will manage the 51-hospital KCIC rural ACO; and the one that already existed and now administers the program, the Care Collaborative. The Year 2 plan makes CIN participation a stated 2031 outcome and funds a statewide commercial rate analysis it describes as the foundation for “joint contract negotiations through clinically integrated networks.” This track carries the networks, their members’ finances, the dollars that build CIN capacity each year, and the plan language, in one place.</p>

<h2 class="serif" id="jump-cins">The networks: Kansas High Value Network, PrairieLINK &amp; Astra Health — members, finances, and RHTP awards</h2>
<div class="sub"><b style="color:#1B8A5A">Astra Health</b> (announced September 8, 2026) is the newest: a shared-services organization formed by Amberwell Health, LMH Health, Newman Regional Health, Salina Regional Health Center, and The University of Kansas Health System, funded by a $932,310 Year 1 RPGP award to Salina Regional for “AstraHealth Kansas shared services organization: benchmarked data, analytics, governance,” and set to provide management services and investments to KCIC — the 51-hospital rural ACO founded in 2016 inside the KU Health System Care Collaborative. The two earlier CINs formed in non-overlapping regions, embodying opposite funding strategies. The <b style="color:var(--sky)">Kansas High Value Network</b> (central / western Kansas), founded by NMC Health of Newton with HaysMed as its tertiary anchor, is an independent-hospital cooperative whose members won individual REH/CAP capital grants; <b style="color:var(--clay)">PrairieLINK Health Alliance</b> (northeast / north-central Kansas) is a single system-led RPGP partnership that also won a post-original-award REH/CAP capital grant for its most distressed member, the Flint Hills campus.
