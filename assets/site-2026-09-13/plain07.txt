<h1 class="serif">The Care Collaborative Association (CCA)</h1>
<p>A wholly owned subsidiary of the University of Kansas Hospital Authority (The University of Kansas Health System), built in 2014 with a $12.4 million CMS Innovation Center award as a rural clinically integrated network. It is the first-tier prime for roughly half of every Kansas RHTP program year, selects its own sub-awardees, and earns a flat monthly administrative fee. This track is computed live from the Year 1 and Year 2 datasets: when a new program year is added, the figures below update themselves.</p>
